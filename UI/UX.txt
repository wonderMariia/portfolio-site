1. Executive Summary
Concept: A “Minimalist Clarity” portfolio tailored for recruiters, hiring managers, and startup leadership. The design emphasizes trust, AI-forward thinking, and human-centered innovation. It is clean, strategic, and conversion-optimized—with no distractions, clear calls to action, and strong visual branding aligned with your professional identity.

8. Accessibility & Performance Checklist
Ensure color contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text and UI elements
Keyboard navigable; form inputs have labels or aria-labels
Lazy-load images (use loading="lazy")
Optimize images: Use WebP, compress <200 kb
Meta viewport, language tag, alt texts
Minimize layout shifts by reserving image space
